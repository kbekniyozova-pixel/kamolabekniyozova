Global Economic Freedom: Governance, Institutions & Market Liberty
University of Denver · Advanced Predictive Analysis with R

Analyzed the Economic Freedom Index across 159 countries (2013–2016) using OLS, Ridge/LASSO regularization, and Random Forest classification in R. Applied K-means clustering to identify country typologies. Demonstrates applied ML methodology in a policy-relevant cross-country context — legal system integrity emerged as the dominant predictive feature over government expenditure size.

Key findings
Random Forest achieved 84% classification accuracy in predicting EFI quartile; legal system integrity was the dominant feature by importance score.
Cluster analysis revealed middle-income countries with large governments but strong property rights — challenging the conventional government-size narrative.

Women’s Education as a Driver of Economic Development in Africa
University of Denver · Modeling for Development

Used the International Futures (IFs) system to evaluate how accelerated investment in women’s education reshapes economic and health trajectories across Africa through 2060. Three counterfactual policy scenarios compared against SDG 3 and SDG 4 benchmarks across six focus countries including Ethiopia, Niger, Mali, and DRC.

Key findings
A 20pp increase in female secondary enrollment produces a compounding GDP-per-capita premium of 12–18% by 2060 relative to baseline.
Quality-adjusted scenario reduced under-5 mortality by an estimated 34 deaths per 1,000 live births beyond the enrollment-only scenario.

Agricultural Liberalization & WTO Compatibility for Uzbekistan’s Accession
Center for Economic Research and Reforms · Policy Research

Applied GTAP computable general equilibrium (CGE) framework and STATA-based regression analysis to model four agricultural liberalization scenarios — from full subsidy elimination to climate-resilient crop reallocation. Evaluated welfare effects, export sector competitiveness, and distributional consequences. Findings directly informed Uzbekistan's WTO accession Working Party submissions and government agricultural reform strategy.

Key findings
Full subsidy elimination yields aggregate welfare gains but disproportionately burdens Fergana Valley smallholders — informing government's preference for a graduated phase-out.
Subsidy reallocation to climate-resilient crops dominated all scenarios on combined welfare and food security metrics, compatible with both WTO commitments and SDG 2.
PythonRegressionVisualization156 Countries
Determinants of Life Expectancy: A Data-Driven Cross-Country Analysis
University of Denver · Python for Data Analytics

Built a full analytical pipeline in Python (pandas, statsmodels, sklearn) to model life expectancy determinants across 156 countries. Applied OLS regression with heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors, VIF diagnostics, and partial regression analysis to quantify the independent effect of income, health expenditure, inequality, education, and clean water access on life outcomes.

Key findings
Final model R² = 0.87 — health system inputs (physician density, immunization) identified as the strongest predictor in low-income countries, outperforming GDP per capita.
Gini coefficient negatively associated with life expectancy even after controlling for income; a 1 SD increase in Gini linked to a 1.8-year reduction.
