RRegressionClassificationPanel Data
Global Economic Freedom: Governance, Institutions & Market Liberty
University of Denver · Advanced Predictive Analysis with R

Empirical investigation into the structural determinants of the Economic Freedom Index across 159 countries (2013–2016) using 32 indicators. Applied OLS, Ridge/LASSO regularization, and Random Forest classification in R to isolate how government size, legal system quality, and property rights interact to predict economic freedom scores.

Key findings
Legal system quality emerged as the strongest predictor of EFI, outperforming government expenditure size.
Random Forest achieved 84% classification accuracy; legal integrity was the dominant feature by importance score.
IFs ModelScenario AnalysisSDGsAfrica
Women’s Education as a Driver of Economic Development in Africa
University of Denver · Modeling for Development

Used the International Futures (IFs) system to evaluate how accelerated investment in women’s education reshapes economic and health trajectories across Africa through 2060. Three counterfactual policy scenarios compared against SDG 3 and SDG 4 benchmarks across six focus countries including Ethiopia, Niger, Mali, and DRC.

Key findings
A 20pp increase in female secondary enrollment produces a compounding GDP-per-capita premium of 12–18% by 2060 relative to baseline.
Quality-adjusted scenario reduced under-5 mortality by an estimated 34 deaths per 1,000 live births beyond the enrollment-only scenario.

Agricultural Liberalization & WTO Compatibility for Uzbekistan’s Accession
Center for Economic Research and Reforms · Policy Research

Analyzed surveys on Uzbekistan’s agricultural export sector and evaluated government subsidies against WTO rules. Modeled four agricultural liberalization scenarios using STATA and GTAP, assessing WTO compatibility, export competitiveness, and distributional consequences from full subsidy elimination to climate-resilient crop reallocation.

Key findings
Full subsidy elimination yields aggregate welfare gains but disproportionately burdens smallholder farmers.
Subsidy reallocation to climate-resilient crops dominated all scenarios on combined welfare and food security metrics.

Determinants of Life Expectancy: A Data-Driven Cross-Country Analysis
University of Denver · Python for Data Analytics

Python-based analysis exploring how economic, social, and health factors influence life expectancy across 156 countries. Full pipeline from data cleaning and EDA through correlation analysis and OLS regression (statsmodels, sklearn) to quantify the independent impact of income, health expenditure, inequality, education, and water access on life outcomes.

Key findings
Health system inputs explain more life expectancy variance than GDP per capita in low-income countries.
Full model: R² = 0.87. Gini coefficient negatively associated with life expectancy even after controlling for income.

Co-authored policy memo assessing the strategic implications of U.S.–Indonesia nickel trade. Key findings

Environmental governance gaps present a DFC financing opportunity linked to ESG conditionality.
